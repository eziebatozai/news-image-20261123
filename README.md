## Stories Covered (23 November 2026)

1. **Tech & Markets (USA / Global):** Nvidia's board unanimously reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO; Nvidia shares jump 31.5% (+$952B) as the Nasdaq climbs 6.5% and the S&P 500 rises 4.7%.
2. **Middle East / Energy (Strait of Hormuz):** Iran's IRGC Navy reports three oil tankers caught fire after hitting naval mines amid the U.S. naval blockade; Brent crude slides 3.5% to $89.4/bbl.
3. **Europe & North Africa (Ukraine / Libya):** President Zelenskyy and Ukraine's SBU confirm seven sea drones sank the Russian corvette *Merkury* at Benghazi naval base in eastern Libya.
4. **Asia / Trade Policy (Singapore / U.S. / China):** U.S. Commerce Department adds Singapore cloud operator Megaspeed and six Southeast Asian affiliates to the Entity List over AI chip diversion risks.
5. **Global Climate (Antalya, Turkey):** World leaders and negotiators react as UN COP31 climate talks collapse without a final overarching agreement over fossil-fuel phaseout language.
6. **Americas / Cybersecurity (USA):** The FBI investigates an apparent cyberattack targeting its nationwide National Crime Information Center (NCIC) law-enforcement database.
7. **Latin America / Security (Cúcuta, Colombia):** A drone attack targeting a police base in Cúcuta injures at least three civilians and damages roughly 60 nearby homes.
8. **South Pacific / Earth Science (Vanuatu):** A deep magnitude 6.5 earthquake strikes at 601 km depth off Vanuatu along the Ring of Fire with no tsunami threat issued.

## Usage

Run the deterministic Node 22 entrypoint locally:

```bash
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Re-render `entry.png` from source (`render.py`, Python 3 + Pillow + NumPy):

```bash
python3 render.py
```
